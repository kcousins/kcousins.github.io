Heavy Breathing

How the respiratory system sets your biological speed limit — a book in progress by Kevin Cousins.

When somebody loses weight, where does the fat go? Most people know the answer for sure: it is burned off as energy. But energy has no mass to carry the atoms away. Follow the atoms with one balanced equation, and most of the fat leaves through the lungs, as carbon dioxide, one breath at a time.

This site holds working drafts of figures and calculations from the book. They are drafts: corrections are welcome.
