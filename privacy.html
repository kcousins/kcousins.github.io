Privacy policy

Personal Google Drive client (rclone). Last updated 21 November 2026.

What this is

This Google Cloud OAuth client is used by one person, Kevin Cousins, to connect rclone on his own computer to his own Google Drive. It is not offered to anyone else, and it has no other users.

What data it accesses

The client asks for access to the Google Drive of the account that signs in. It reads and writes files in that Drive only when its owner runs rclone, to copy, sync or mount his own files.

What happens to the data

Files move directly between Google Drive and the owner's computer. No data is sent to any other server, collected, stored elsewhere, sold or shared. The OAuth token is kept only in the rclone configuration file on the owner's computer.

Use of Google user data

This client's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
